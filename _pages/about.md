I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at the University of Santiago, Chile. I am an applied microeconomist with research interests in Development Economics and Labor Economics. My current research agenda focuses on the role of social interactions in the economics of crime.

I hold a Ph.D. and a Master of Economics from Georgetown University, as well as a B.Sc. in Economics (Hons) and a Master of Financial Economics (Hons) from the University of Santiago, Chile.

**Primary fields:** Applied Microeconomics, Development Economics, Crime

**Seconday field:** Labor
